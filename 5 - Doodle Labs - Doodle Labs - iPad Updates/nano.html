Performance
Long Range
Doodle Labs radios are engineered for extreme range performance, with field-proven links exceeding 300 kilometers. Whether ground-to-air or air-to-air, our solutions ensure uninterrupted connectivity across expansive and dynamic environments.
Anti-Jamming
With advanced interference avoidance and dynamic spectrum agility, our radios are built to operate reliably in contested and congested RF environments. These capabilities make them ideal for defense, public safety, and industrial applications where signal integrity is mission-critical.
Mesh Networking
Every Doodle Labs radio supports a robust mesh architecture—self-forming, self-healing, and optimized for ultra-low latency. This enables resilient, decentralized networks that keep teams, sensors, and autonomous platforms connected without relying on fixed infrastructure.
